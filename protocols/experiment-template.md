## Procedure

1. Set up equipment in a low-background area.
2. Perform and record background spectrum (minimum 5–10 minutes recommended).
3. Position source and detector at first measurement point.
4. Acquire spectrum for defined live time.
5. Export data (note filename convention).
6. Record all metadata in this protocol or linked spreadsheet.
7. Move to next distance/geometry/shielding configuration.
8. Repeat background check periodically.
9. Dismantle setup safely.
